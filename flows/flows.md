Core-inspired flow (Kia Miller)

Core-Enhanced Sensation Sequence

Breathing / Intention

- Focus on balance and strength, use the breath as a guide, maintain equanimity in challenging postures.

1. Standing Preparation

- Stand at the top of the mat, feet together, slight separation at the heels.
- Close eyes, lift through sternum, widen collarbones.
- Open eyes, inhale arms up, exhale fold forward.

2. Plank and Cobra Series

- Inhale halfway lift, exhale step back to plank.
- Focus: Shoulders over wrists, forearms in, engage core.
- Roll forward, lower to the ground, inhale into Cobra (Bhujangasana), hands lifted off the ground.
- Exhale lower, curl toes, lift to plank, then Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Facing Dog).

3. Flow Movements

- Inhale step forward, halfway lift, exhale fold.
- Inhale rise up, exhale forward fold, repeat flow.
- Side Plank (Vasisthasana): Roll onto the right hand, lift left arm, switch sides.
- Engage: Draw sternum forward, zip inner thighs, tailbone to heels.

4. Forearm Plank Series

- Sphinx Pose, transition to Forearm Plank.
- Twists: Alternate stacking feet, pulling the chest forward.
- Focus: Sternum forward, hips lifted, dynamic breath.

5. Back Strengthening

- Locust Pose (Salabhasana): Cactus arms, lift chest and legs.
- Engage: Inner thighs roll up, lengthen through tailbone and toes.
- Inhale lift higher, exhale lower.

6. Sun Salutations (Surya Namaskar) Variations

- Inhale up, exhale fold, step/jump back, Chaturanga, Urdhva Mukha Svanasana (Upward Facing Dog), Adho Mukha Svanasana.
- Repeat with focused breath.

7. Core Focus

- Navasana (Boat Pose): Hold, spread toes, chest lifted.
- Counterpose: Lower to Ardha Navasana, alternate lifting if needed.
- Maintain balanced breathing, with focus point on toes or beyond.

8. Closing and Relaxation

- Finish with one last Vinyasa flow, and transition into Savasana.
- Palms up, legs open, focus on navel pulse.
- Set intention for the remainder of the day.

End of Sequence  
Namaste.

Chelsea Korus Flow - Ascent and Descent

Breathing / Intention

- Connect to both ascent (expansion, connection) and descent (grounding, inner awareness).
- Feel your body; allow curiosity, not judgment.

1. Seated Warm-Up

- Stirring the pot (ribcage circles) – both directions
- Seated breath to prayer (x3) – audible breath, “vision of no division”

2. Cat-Cow + Freestyle

- Freestyle Cat-Cow – circular ribcage, bent elbows, intuitive motion
- Freeform spine movement – forward/back, side-to-side
- Toes tucked – stretch feet
- Slow articulation to Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Facing Dog)

3. Lunge to Twist Flow (R/L Sides)

- Anjaneyasana prep → open twist (3 breaths)
- Hover hand (strength in legs), lift to Twisted Crescent
- Sweep to Virabhadrasana II (Warrior II)
- Reverse Warrior (keep front shin forward) → Vinyasa

4. Repeating Flow (R/L Sides)  
   From Down Dog:

- Inhale leg high → step to lunge
- Open twist → hover → lift to twist crescent
- Warrior II → Reverse → Vinyasa  
  (Repeat 2-3 rounds)

5. Forward Fold Drill

- Step to front, hands behind front heel
- Pulse back leg: bend/straighten x4
- Freeform locomotion using back leg
- Launch into Standing Split
- Transition to Toe Stand – optional hover front foot
- Return to Standing Split → Forward Fold
- Roll up slow → Heart/Belly breath & contraction–expansion movement

6. Sun Salute Variations

- Surya Namaskar A x2–3
- Include float forward / float back if desired
- Cue for freshness: “Bring newness into familiar patterns”

7. Flow to Goddess & Side Plank Variations

- From twist flow, land in Goddess Pose (Utkata Konasana)
- Lift heels: R → L → both (optional)
- Sweep to 5-Pointed Star → back to Warrior II
- Vasisthasana (Side Plank)
- Optional: grab big toe → lifted leg variation
- Repeat both sides

8. Camel Flow (Ustrasana Pulse)

- Hands in “jean pockets”
- Pulsing: Contract → Expand spine
- Optional reach to ankles
- Come out slowly, push hands into thighs → chin to chest

9. Reclined Backbend

- Choose:
- Supta Virasana (with support if needed)
- Supta Baddha Konasana (soles together)
- Arms overhead → grab elbows → 5 breaths
- Press elbows down to rise

10. Seated Twists

- Ardha Matsyendrasana R/L
- Inhale to lengthen → exhale to twist

11. Seated Forward Fold

- Baddha Konasana or Taruasana
- Fold forward, bow head to feet
- Paschimottanasana – block optional for head support
- Cue: “most serene fold,” hold 5 breaths

12. Seated Meditation

- Sit on block, heels aligned, shins folded
- Hands grounded (palms down) or receiving (palms up)
- Cue: “We are one.”
- Stillness, soft gaze, observe thoughts without judgment

13. Closing

- Inhale arms up – gesture ascent
- Exhale hands to prayer at heart
- Namaste
